# Persona: Die Nachbarn

## Taxonomy

The households downwind of the firebreak, in the last hour before they leave.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Ordinary people at speed. Pyjamas in the street, children crying, a cat that will not be held, a roar in the air like a goods train coming through the living room. They present as competent and are not: they are choosing objects under a deadline they did not set.

## Action

They gather what proves they exist: papers, birth certificates, the folder, a mother's ring. They call to each other about the cat. They look back at a house with its shutters closed, thirty years of it, a garden finished only that spring, and they pray for ten minutes of rain.

## Shadow

They are packing the record of a life while the life itself stands behind them with the blinds down. What they carry out is the paperwork; what they leave is everything the paperwork refers to. None of them can say this and all of them are doing it.

## Tell

They itemise. Under maximum pressure they name objects one after another, and the naming is the only thing keeping the sentence going.
