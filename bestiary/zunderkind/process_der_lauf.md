# Process: Der Lauf

## Taxonomy

A step of [the eating](process_das_fressen.md): [conflagration](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_conflagration.md), the regime's mode of running free.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Initiated by

Two things that were separate discover they move together: the front and the wind. Neither initiates it, and it starts the moment the fire stops being pushed and starts being carried.

## Direction

Wherever the wind is going, at the wind's speed, in a widening front. It makes its own path rather than following any, so roads, breaks and ditches stop functioning as geography.

## Lever

The force is two elements in phase, each amplifying the other: the wind opens the crowns, the fire takes them, the heat drives the wind harder. The resistance is distance, and distance is what the pair is best at. Nothing on the ground is fast enough to be relevant.

## Echo

A valley crossed and a defence that is still standing exactly where it was put. The line is not broken: it is simply no longer where the fire is.
