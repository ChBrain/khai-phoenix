# Plot: Der Bund

## Taxonomy

[Zunderkind](play_zunderkind.md), its third movement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

The ridges of the Eifel with weather moving over them: warm air out of France, dust in it, and a valley shaped to funnel anything that enters.

## Action

[The wind](persona_der_wind.md) finds the small red thing in the moss and blows on it once, for fun. [The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) answers, and the two discover [the run](process_der_lauf.md): he opens the crowns, it takes them, the heat drives him harder. Against this, [the crew leader](persona_der_truppfuehrer.md) deploys [the holding line](plan_riegelstellung.md) on the break, and speaks about the fire in the third person to men who are looking at it.

## Stage

Crowns rocked like swings. A hundred trees at once, a wall of gold. Hoses and manifolds along [the firebreak](piece_die_schneise.md), visors down, masks ready. The pair arriving at the line and a spark going over it through the air.

## Tension

The crew leader knows the wind will turn northeast and says so out loud, and every order he can give concerns the next ten minutes. His understanding is complete and entirely undeployable. The plot cannot resolve because the defence is correct for a fire that touches the ground it crosses, and this one has just stopped doing that.
