# Place: Der Wald

## Taxonomy

The spruce stands along the ridges of the Eifel, three hundred hectares of them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Shown

It shows a plantation: one species, one age, planted in rows, closed canopy, and dead wood lying everywhere between the trunks. Needles cured to biscuit. Resin. No gaps and no wet.

What it carries is [exclusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_exclusion.md): this is ground that has been kept from burning, and it presents the result of that as abundance. The fuel it shows a visitor is the fuel it was not allowed to spend.

## Holds

[The firebreak](piece_die_schneise.md). Dead wood, everywhere, at every step. Cones. Moss with something red and yellow glittering in it. Cured needle litter, ankle deep.

## Offers

Passage, shade, timber, walking. It offers a fire everything it needs and asks nothing in return: every visitor is given full access to the fuel, and nothing in the place refuses anyone.

## Withheld

A gap. There is nowhere in it that will not carry, so it withholds the one thing that would let anybody stop a fire inside it: somewhere for the fire to fail. It gives that up only to machinery, cut by hand, and even then only as a line on the ground.
