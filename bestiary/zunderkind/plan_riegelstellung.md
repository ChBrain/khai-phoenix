# Plan: Riegelstellung

## Taxonomy

A mandate: the containment scheme the fireground is directed at, holding a sector on a cut line.

## Owner

- Owner: [Der Truppführer](persona_der_truppfuehrer.md)

## Direction

Stop the spread at [the firebreak](piece_die_schneise.md) and hold the sector, so that what is behind the line stays behind it. The plan is sound for a fire that travels along the ground, and it is executed correctly throughout.

## Orders

- To the crews: manifold three, water on the second line, move.
- To the sector: hold the break, not one metre further.
- To everyone: watch the roots, visors down, masks ready.

## Implementation

On the cut line, with what is on the trucks. The plan assumes the fire arrives at the line, which is what fires do, and it has no step for a fire that arrives past it.
