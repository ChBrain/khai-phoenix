# Persona: Der Truppführer

## Taxonomy

The fireground at its working end: the man holding a sector on the fourth day, named here in prose.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Soot in the pores and past performing. Since Thursday morning even his spit tastes of burnt wood. He speaks in radio traffic and in the flat obscenity of people who have stopped being polite, and he is entirely accurate about everything he says.

## Action

He holds the firebreak and runs [the containment](plan_riegelstellung.md): manifold three, water on the second line, visors down, masks ready. He warns his crew about the roots. He tells them what happens when the wind turns northeast.

## Shadow

He is the only figure who understands the situation completely and the only one who can do nothing about it. His competence has been converted into accurate prediction, which is the most useless form it can take. He knows the wind will turn, he says so out loud, and he holds the line anyway.

## Tell

He gives orders about the next ten minutes and forecasts about the next ten hours, and the two do not meet. Nothing he commands can affect anything he predicts.
