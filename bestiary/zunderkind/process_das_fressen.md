# Process: Das Fressen

## Taxonomy

[Ignition](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_ignition.md) carried on into growth: the threshold mode of the regime, read here as the entity's only way of becoming anything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Initiated by

Heat meets fuel cured dry enough to take, and something is alight that was only combustible before. In this production it starts in needle litter and the first mouthful is also the first sentence.

## Direction

Outward and upward, by appetite: needle, leaf, cone, branch, crown, valley. It has no other mode of increase, so it grows exactly as fast as it consumes and in the same shape.

## Lever

The force is hunger in fuel that offers no resistance at all; the resistance is only the size of the mouth. That is why it accelerates: every mouthful enlarges the mouth, and nothing in [the forest](place_der_wald.md) declines.

## Echo

An entity where there was a spark, and a hectare of absence behind it. What it has eaten is what it now is, which is the production's whole argument and the reason it can say I at the end.
