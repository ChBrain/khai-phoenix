# Pitch: Die Kinderstimme

## Taxonomy

The key of [Zunderkind](play_zunderkind.md), and the only production in the house tuned to delight.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Tenor

Delight, in short sentences. Everything is new, everything is edible, and the forest is addressed directly as a playmate. Diminutives, direct questions, sound words. The entity has no word for harm and no reason to look for one.

## Undertow

Everything it says is true and every consequence is catastrophic. The reader holds both at once, and the register never acknowledges the second. The gap widens as the production runs and is never once closed from inside the voice.

## Nerve

The dread of watching something be exactly as harmless as it believes it is. It plays for the reader's inability to be angry at it.

## Echo

That the appetite and the self arrived together, and that nothing was done to it to make it that way.
