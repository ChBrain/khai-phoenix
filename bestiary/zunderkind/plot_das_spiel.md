# Plot: Das Spiel

## Taxonomy

[Zunderkind](play_zunderkind.md), its second movement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

Deeper into [the forest](place_der_wald.md), still nobody about. Sun on resin, everything dry to the ground, the stand so uniform that one metre of it predicts the next hundred.

## Action

[The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) plays: it tastes the difference between a needle and a leaf and a cone, it climbs, it invites the wood to hold hands. [The eating](process_das_fressen.md) accelerates without being driven, because every mouthful makes the next one easier. It is not trying to grow and it grows.

## Stage

Branches bent toward each other, cones, a mouthful of summer and honey and resin. The entity moving from a cup to an armful, and no witness to mark the change.

## Tension

The forest cannot decline. [The place](place_der_wald.md) withholds nothing and offers everything, so there is no moment where the game could be refused and no point at which the entity learns the word no. What makes the movement impossible to complete cleanly is the absence of any resistance at all: a game needs two, and only one party is playing.
