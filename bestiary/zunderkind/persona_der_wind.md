# Persona: Der Wind

## Taxonomy

The second element, a partner and never a servant. Native to this production alone: every later beast burns where he cannot reach, and his absence there is a statement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Careless and enormous. He arrives over the ridges with no luggage, warm breath out of France and a dusty mood, and he treats the whole valley as somewhere to pass through. He bends crowns for the pleasure of bending them.

## Action

He finds the small red thing glowing in the moss and blows on it, gently, once. He goes ahead and rocks the treetops, bends branches toward each other, and turns northeast when he feels like it. He does not lead the fire and he does not follow it: they discover they are two legs of one stride.

## Shadow

He believes he is playing. He is the only figure who could stop the production by simply going elsewhere, and it never occurs to him that his attention is a decision. The turn to northeast at the end is a whim to him and a verdict on a village to everyone downwind.

## Tell

He announces arrivals and never departures. He says where he is going next and never what he left behind.
