# Piece: Die Schneise

## Taxonomy

The cut lines through [the forest](place_der_wald.md): breaks, forest roads, and the cleared strips beside them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Place

Cut through the stand below the ridge, running across the slope, with the crews and their manifolds deployed along it.

## Load Bearing

It is the assumption the defence rests on, drawn in the ground. A firebreak is a bet that fire travels by touching, and everything the crews do depends on that bet: hold the line and the fire stops. Remove it and there is no defence to lose, and no moment where the fire is seen to be a different kind of thing than everyone thought.

## Apparent

A strip of bare ground, maybe twenty metres, spoil pushed to both sides. Hoses along it. Trodden heather. From the ground it looks like a decision that has been made and cannot be unmade.

## Yearbook

Cut years ago in a wetter decade, widened once after a scare, and maintained on a schedule. Through the operation it held: the crews were placed on it, the manifolds were set on it, and it was the only ground anyone was confident about. On the third day it was crossed in one second by a spark that went over it through the air, and the crews stayed on it afterwards because there was nowhere better to stand.
