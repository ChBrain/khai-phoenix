# Plot: Das Erwachen

## Taxonomy

[Zunderkind](play_zunderkind.md), its first movement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

[The forest](place_der_wald.md) at the end of a dry summer: cured needle litter ankle deep, dead wood between the trunks, resin in the air, and the moss holding something red and yellow that nobody is looking at.

## Action

[The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) is suddenly present where nothing was, and finds out what it is by eating: [the eating](process_das_fressen.md) begins in the same instant as the awareness, and the two never separate. It crawls onto a branch, one toe first, then the whole belly. Nobody opposes it because nobody is there.

## Stage

The needle litter, the branch, the moss. The entity alone, small enough to fit in a cup, blowing spark bubbles at an audience it assumes exists.

## Tension

It is asking the forest to play and describing it as food in the same sentence, and it hears no contradiction. That gap is the plot's whole engine: nothing external threatens it, so the only thing complicating the movement is that its affection and its appetite are the same faculty and it cannot tell them apart.
