# Persona: Das Wesen

## Taxonomy

The fire itself, newborn. The same entity every later beast is, before it has a type.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Bright, delighted, and small enough to fit in a cup. It shows itself as a playmate: it invites, it asks questions, it points things out. It describes what it eats by taste rather than by mass, and it does not know that anything it says is frightening.

## Action

It eats, and by eating it grows: needle, leaf, cone, branch, then a hundred trees at once. It blows spark bubbles. It invites the forest to play. It takes the wind's hand and runs. At the end it stops asking and says [I](process_das_fressen.md) instead.

## Shadow

It cannot tell the difference between company and food, and it says so in the same breath without hearing it: it means no harm and it means to eat everything. It also cannot see that it is being assembled out of what it consumes, so it experiences growth as joy rather than as a cost paid by something else.

## Tell

It offers affection in the vocabulary of appetite. Every endearment it uses is a word about eating, and it never notices the substitution.
