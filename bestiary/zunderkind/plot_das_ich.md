# Plot: Das Ich

## Taxonomy

[Zunderkind](play_zunderkind.md), its fourth movement and its close.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

Three valleys of [forest](place_der_wald.md) alight at once, under a smoke column that has stopped being weather and become a lid. The light is its own now. Thunder gathering in a cloud nothing else made.

## Action

[The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) stops inviting. [The eating](process_das_fressen.md) has run to its end and produced something with a body that spans three valleys and a pulse in the beat of bursting wood. [The neighbours](persona_die_nachbarn.md) run the other way with the paperwork of their lives. [The crew leader](persona_der_truppfuehrer.md) holds a line that is no longer between anything.

## Stage

Pyjamas in the street, a folder, a ring, a cat. Closed shutters on a house with a garden finished that spring. Trenches scratched in the dust. Ten square kilometres pulling in against a single chest.

## Tension

Two namings arrive in the same week and neither party hears the other. Everyone downwind has known what this is for days and has been saying it into radios and telephones; the entity learns it in the last line and says it as if it were news. The plot ends on the one word the whole production was assembling, spoken by the only figure who did not know it was being assembled.
