# Play: Zunderkind

## Estate

[khai-phoenix](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-phoenix), the Phoenix: the bestiary
of fire that holds this run and answers for it.

This is the one production in the house with **no combustion spine**, and the
exception is definitional rather than loose: Zunderkind is fire before it is a
kind of fire. It casts the regime only, from the
[fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_fire.md) engine: ignition, creep, and
conflagration, with no named phenomenon underneath. Every other beast in the
house takes its spine from combustion, because a type is what combustion names.
This one has nothing to name yet.

## Name

**Zunderkind.** The birth. It is billed as the first production of the cycle and
the last one that could still have become anything, and every later beast is
this entity wearing a form.

## Arc

It wakes without knowing what it is, and it finds out by eating.

The bend is that becoming and consuming are the same act here. It plays: the
needle tastes of biscuit, the branch tickles, the forest is a companion it invites
to play. It grows only by taking, and every mouthful makes it larger and less
able to stop. The wind arrives and it learns partnership, which doubles it. Then
it crosses the line where the thing it was playing with is simply gone, and the
people who name it are speaking a language it never hears.

At the end of growing, there is something present that can say _I_. That sentence
is the whole point of the production and it arrives only because everything else
was consumed to build it. Reordered, this is a fire getting bigger. In order, it
is a self being assembled out of a forest.

## Company

The closed cast. A production draws from here and no further.

- **das Wesen**, the fire, speaking: newborn, delighted, and without a type. It is
  the same entity that appears in every later beast, before it has a form to
  lose.
- **der Wind**, the second element: a partner, never a servant, arriving with warm
  breath out of France and dusty spirits. He is native to this production only.
  Later beasts burn where he cannot reach, and his absence there is a statement
  rather than an omission.
- **der Truppführer**, the fireground: soot in the pores, radio traffic, and the
  flat exhausted speech of the fourth day. He knows what it is. He never speaks
  to it.
- **die Nachbarn**, the people downwind: papers, birth certificates, a mother's
  ring, a cat, a garden finished in spring.
- **der Wald**, the place: spruce monoculture across the ridges of the Eifel, dry
  to the ground, three hundred hectares of it.
- **die Schneise**, the piece: the firebreak, a line drawn on the assumption that
  fire travels along the ground.
- **Riegelstellung**, the plan: the containment scheme, held by the fireground,
  whose targets are set at the state they stand in when the curtain rises.
- **die Kinderstimme**, the pitch: the default key. Everything is described by a
  voice that has no word for harm.

Files for these land with the board; here they are named, not linked.

## Triggers

Four plots, each closing on the next one's cue.

1. **Das Erwachen.** It is suddenly there, bright where dark was, with fingers of
   light and an appetite. Cue: dark becomes light in the needle litter. Closes
   when it has eaten enough to move, which fires:
2. **Das Spiel.** It plays with the forest: crawls the branch, blows spark
   bubbles, invites the wood to play. This is the only movement where the
   invitation is sincere. Closes when the wind finds it, which fires:
3. **Der Bund.** Two elements discover they are two legs of a single stride. It
   runs, it crowns, it jumps the Schneise on a flying spark. The people appear and
   speak about it in the third person. Closes when the wind turns to north-east,
   which fires:
4. **Das Ich.** The body spans three valleys, the smoke stops being haze and
   becomes its own sky, thunder gathers in the cloud it made. It stops asking the
   forest to play. Closes on the sentence: ICH BIN.

## Stakes

**Werden:** whether there comes to be anyone there at all.

Nothing in this production is fighting over territory or survival, which is what
makes it unlike every beast that follows. The fire is never in danger and never
knows it is dangerous. What is contested is whether an appetite becomes a self,
and the forest is what gets spent to settle it.

Every plot moves it. Das Erwachen supplies an appetite with no owner. Das Spiel
gives it a relationship, which is the first thing it can be a self toward. Der
Bund gives it a partner and therefore a first person plural, _wir_, which is the
step before _ich_. Das Ich spends the last of the forest and arrives at the
pronoun. The people and their Riegelstellung move it too, from the other side:
they name it long before it can name itself, and the production's cruelty is that
their name and its name arrive in the same week.
