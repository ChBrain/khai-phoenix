# Play: Moorleiche

## Estate

[khai-phoenix](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-phoenix), the Phoenix: the bestiary
of fire that holds this run and answers for it. This is the first beast with a
type, and the first production in which the entity has something to lose.

Its spine is [peat fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_peat.md), the
wildland phenomenon of a smouldering peat bed that burns downward and unseen for
months. It leans on [smoulder](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_smoulder.md) and
[exclusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_exclusion.md) from the regime, on
[holdover](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_holdover.md) for the
overwintering, and on
[renewal](@chbrain/khai-composite-blaze/process_blaze_renewal.md) for the ending
that begins again.

## Name

**Moorleiche.** The bog body: what the peat keeps, preserved and unrotted, long
after everyone has stopped looking. The name is billed over a production about
being declared dead and being wrong about it in both directions.

## Arc

It was defeated, and it survives by becoming something that cannot be defeated
the same way, and it pays for that with everything that made it worth defeating.

The bend is that the victory over it is the condition of its second life. Above
ground it was wings, gold, and thunder. The water takes all of it, the crowns go
cold, and someone puts up a board with a date. What goes into the earth is
smaller in every dimension except duration. Down there it scratches out its own
eyes because light is no longer useful, and it learns to eat crumb by crumb what
it used to swallow whole.

Then the paradox turns. They drive steel lances into the ground and inject steam,
and every shaft aerates its cavities, dries its flanks, and cuts the galleries it
will crawl along. The attack is the infrastructure. It sits out December, January,
the long February, listening to the ice crack overhead, and when the wind swings
east in spring it comes up through a crack in the moss as a grey thread with no
flame in it at all.

Reordered, this is a fire that hid. In order, it is a fire that was buried alive
by people who were sure, and that used their certainty as a building material.

## Company

The closed cast. A production draws from here and no further.

- **das Wesen**, the fire, speaking: the same entity as the birth, now typed, and
  it remembers. Blind by choice, patient, chewing.
- **der Truppführer**, the fireground: competent, tired, and finished. He does the
  correct thing at every step.
- **die Einsatzleitung**, the office: the position that decides a fire is out and
  signs a date to it.
- **die Spaziergänger**, the people on the heath: dogs, photographs of the mist,
  and a preference for the snow-free strips of moss where the ground is warm and
  soft underfoot.
- **das Moor**, the place: the wet corpses of ancient forests, layered, iron in
  the mouth, and warm at depth all winter.
- **die Stahllanze**, the piece: the injection lance, hundred-degree steam, driven
  down to kill and building tunnels instead.
- **die Birke**, the piece: a birch upright in the frost beside the boardwalk,
  blind to the fact that its roots are already white ash.
- **Löschen**, the plan: the suppression scheme, run by the Einsatzleitung, whose
  targets settle above ground and never below.
- **Überwintern**, the plan: the entity's own scheme, which is simply to last
  until the wind swings east.
- **die Kalte Stimme**, the pitch: the default key. It reports. It is neither
  monster nor victim.

Files for these land with the board; here they are named, not linked.

## Triggers

Four plots, each closing on the next one's cue.

1. **Das Ertrinken.** Tons of water from the air, hoses out of the dust, the roar
   choked into white steam. Cue: the crowns go cold. Closes when the board goes up
   with a date on it, which fires:
2. **Der Abstieg.** It goes down into the peat and rebuilds itself for the dark:
   eyes out, appetite reduced, crumb by crumb, layer by layer. Closes when the
   people above decide the ground still needs treating, which fires:
3. **Die Gabe.** The lances come down. Steam hisses out of the wounds of the
   ground, every shaft opens a vein, and the attack becomes the tunnel system. The
   walkers photograph the mist and keep to the warm strips. Closes when winter
   shuts the surface, which fires:
4. **Die Rückkehr.** December, January, the thick February, ice cracking overhead.
   The wind turns east, spring pulls the last wet out of the upper stalks, and a
   soundless grey thread lifts from a crack in the moss. Closes on the red hands
   closing around the birch.

## Stakes

**Fortdauer:** whether this fire continues as itself.

The contest is asymmetrical and that is the point. The people are fighting to
finish something and they have procedures, equipment, and a date. The entity is
fighting to persist and has only its type, which is very bad at everything except
lasting. Nobody in the production is incompetent and the wrong side wins.

Every plot moves it. Das Ertrinken settles that it cannot continue in the form it
had. Der Abstieg buys continuance at the price of everything else. Die Gabe is
where the stake inverts: the act meant to end it extends it, and the people who
performed that act will never learn they did. Die Rückkehr collects, and the birch
is the receipt.
