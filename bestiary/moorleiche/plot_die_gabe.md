# Plot: Die Gabe

## Taxonomy

[Moorleiche](play_moorleiche.md), its third movement, and the turn the play is built on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

[The moor](place_das_moor.md) in late summer, the incident formally closed but the ground still on the list. Autumn is coming in: hoarfrost on the heather like icing sugar, a fog worth photographing, the boardwalk wet.

## Action

[Incident command](position_einsatzleitung.md) orders the ground treated, and [the crew leader](persona_der_truppfuehrer.md) carries out the last step of [the suppression](plan_loeschen.md) exactly as written. [The aeration](process_belueftung.md) runs: every shaft opens a channel, dries its own walls, and hands [the entity](persona_das_wesen.md) a route where compacted peat used to be. Meanwhile [the walkers](persona_die_spaziergaenger.md) cross the same ground and choose their path by its warmth.

## Stage

[The lances](piece_die_stahllanze.md) driven into the bed on a two-metre grid, steam hissing out of each puncture. Dogs, cameras, the boardwalk. [The birch](piece_die_birke.md) upright in the frost beside it. The snow-free strips of moss, which everyone walks on and nobody reads.

## Tension

Nobody present is wrong about anything they can measure, and that is what makes the scene impossible to complete cleanly. The crew leader carries a treatment he believes is a killing. The walkers carry a preference for warm ground that is, unknown to them, a perfectly accurate survey of the fire's extent. The entity carries the one thing that would make sense of all of it, and it has no way to tell anyone, and would not if it could.
