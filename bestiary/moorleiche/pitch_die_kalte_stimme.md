# Pitch: Die Kalte Stimme

## Taxonomy

The key of [Moorleiche](play_moorleiche.md), and the default key of the Phoenix house wherever a beast has a type to report from.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Tenor

Reportage from inside a mechanism. The entity states what it does and what is done to it, in order, without appeal. It is neither monster nor victim, and it never argues its case: it describes conditions, rates, and depths. The register is quiet because the thing describing is quiet.

## Undertow

Grief it will not name. It remembers being the wall of gold and mentions it once, flatly, then does not return to it. The refusal to mourn out loud is what makes the reduction land: the reader does the mourning the entity declines to do.

## Nerve

The moment the reader understands that everyone above ground is competent, correct, and wrong. It presses the specific dread of a procedure carried out perfectly on the wrong layer.

## Echo

That the ground is not finished with you. A reader should walk on soft warm moss afterwards and think about what is under it.
