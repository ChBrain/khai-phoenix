# Persona: Der Truppführer

## Taxonomy

[The incident command](position_einsatzleitung.md), at the working end of it: the man who holds the sector and signs that it is out.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Competent and finished. Soot in the pores, a fourth day behind him, the flat register of someone who has stopped performing for anyone. He presents the job as done because by every measure available to him it is done: the crowns are cold, the steam has stopped, the board is up with a date on it.

## Action

He runs [the suppression](plan_loeschen.md) to its end: water on the crowns, then lances into the ground, then the sector closed and signed. He wipes the soot off, he smiles, he puts up the board. He goes home.

## Shadow

He thinks the fire is a thing that happens above ground, and every instrument he owns agrees with him. He cannot conceive of the ground itself as the fire's body, so the lances read to him as treatment rather than as excavation. He will never learn he was wrong: nothing in his world reports back from below, and the re-emergence in spring will be filed as a new fire.

## Tell

He treats the ground as floor. He kicks it, stands on it, drives things into it, and never once listens to it. The confidence of the boot is the leak.
