# Plan: Überwintern

## Taxonomy

A personal scheme: the entity's own, and the simplest plan in the production. It has one idea.

## Owner

- Owner: [Das Wesen](persona_das_wesen.md)

## Direction

Last until the wind swings east. Not to win, not to spread, not to be seen: to still be burning in April, at which point the season does the rest. Every choice it makes is subordinated to duration, including the choice to stop being anything worth looking at.

## Orders

- To itself: give up light, give up speed, give up the surface. Eat crumb by crumb, layer by layer, never faster than the air allows.
- To itself: accept [what the lances open](process_belueftung.md) without understanding it as a gift, and use the galleries.
- To itself: do not surface while the ground is wet or the ice is on it.

## Implementation

Below ground, in [the moor](place_das_moor.md), at depth, through December, January, and the thick February. The only instrument is patience and the only measurement is the sound of ice cracking overhead.

## Targets

- [x] Go into the earth rather than out
- [x] Blind itself: light is no longer useful
- [x] Reduce intake to what the oxygen at depth allows
- [ ] Hold through the winter
- [ ] Surface when the wind turns east and the upper stalks dry
- [ ] Take [the birch](piece_die_birke.md)
