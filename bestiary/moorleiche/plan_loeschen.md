# Plan: Löschen

## Taxonomy

A mandate: the scheme [incident command](position_einsatzleitung.md) is directed at, run to bring a wildland incident to a recorded close.

## Owner

- Owner: [Die Einsatzleitung](position_einsatzleitung.md)

## Direction

Bring the incident to an end that can be signed, on the schedule the deployment allows. Cold crowns, a treated ground, released crews, a date on a board. The plan is competent, correct in every step, and aimed one layer above where the fire is.

## Orders

- To [the crew leader](persona_der_truppfuehrer.md): hold the sector, put water on the crowns until the flame is out, then treat the ground with [the lances](piece_die_stahllanze.md) on a two-metre grid.
- To the crews: three passes, then pull, clean, rack.
- To the recorder: enter the closure with the date.

## Implementation

Above ground, by instrument and by eye. Success criteria are surface criteria: no flame, no visible smoke, no measurable heat at the surface. Nothing in the plan requires anyone to open the bed and look, because opening the bed is what the lances are believed to accomplish.

## Targets

- [x] Water on the crowns until the flame is out
- [x] Steam and water injected across the northern sector, three passes
- [x] Sector declared cold, board raised, 14 July
- [x] Crews released
- [F] The fire ended
- [W] Ground monitored through the winter
