# Plot: Die Rückkehr

## Taxonomy

[Moorleiche](play_moorleiche.md), its fourth movement and its close.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

[The moor](place_das_moor.md) through a long winter and out the other side: December, January, the thick February, ice cracking over the bed. Then the wind swings east and the spring sun pulls the last wet out of the upper stalks.

## Action

[The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) completes [its scheme](plan_ueberwintern.md): it has lasted, which was the whole plan, and now it surfaces. [The smouldering](process_schwelen.md) reaches the root system it has been working toward all winter. A soundless grey thread lifts from a fine crack in the moss, no smoke that burns, only a foul breath out of the depth. Nobody is running any counter-vector at all: the incident is closed, the crews are elsewhere, and the plan that opposed it has been filed.

## Stage

The cracked moss, the thread, and [the birch](piece_die_birke.md) upright beside the boardwalk. No crews, no lances, no instruments. The entity pushes the cold earth aside and its red hands close around the tree.

## Tension

The birch is standing and healthy by every visible measure, and its roots have been white ash for months. That contradiction is the contraband the whole production has been carrying: the surface has been reporting a state that stopped being true in autumn. When the tree goes, nothing about it will look like a continuation of the July fire, and it will be recorded as a new one, which means the entity wins the argument and no one will ever know there was an argument.
