# Persona: Die Spaziergänger

## Taxonomy

The public on the heath: dog walkers, photographers of mist, people out on a cold bright afternoon.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Untroubled. They present the landscape as recreation: hoarfrost on the heather like icing sugar, a photogenic fog, a boardwalk, a birch. They are the only figures in the production with nothing at stake and no idea there is a production.

## Action

They walk [the moor](place_das_moor.md), photograph the mist, and keep to the snow-free strips of moss where the ground is warm and soft underfoot.

## Shadow

They read warmth as comfort. The bare strips are the most legible sign in the whole landscape of what is happening below, and they follow them precisely because they are pleasant to walk on. Their instinct for comfort is a perfect instrument for locating the fire, pointed at nothing.

## Tell

They choose their path by the temperature of the ground and never remark on it.
