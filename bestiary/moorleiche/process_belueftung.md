# Process: Die Belüftung

## Taxonomy

A step of [the smouldering](process_schwelen.md): what happens to the bed when it is treated, and the paradox the production turns on.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Initiated by

[A lance](piece_die_stahllanze.md) is driven into the bed to inject water or steam. The intent is irrelevant to the mechanism: what matters is that a rigid shaft has been opened through a compacted, oxygen-starved layer.

## Direction

Toward more air at depth. Each shaft is a channel; each channel dries its own walls as the injected heat passes; each dried wall is easier to burn than the wet peat around it. The treatment propagates the conditions the burn was short of.

## Lever

The force is the crew's certainty, applied with equipment that works exactly as designed. The resistance is the bed's own density, which was the only thing limiting the burn. The shaft defeats the resistance, and the treatment wins against the wrong opponent.

## Echo

A network of galleries where there was compacted peat, and a fire with a route system it did not build. Nothing above ground records the change.
