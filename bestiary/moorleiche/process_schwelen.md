# Process: Das Schwelen

## Taxonomy

[Peat fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_peat.md), the spine of this production: the flameless, oxygen-limited burn of an organic bed, taken here as the entity's own way of eating.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Initiated by

Flame is removed from the surface while heat remains in the layer beneath it. Where the bed is deep, organic, and dry enough at depth, the burn does not stop when the fire above is put out: it continues without flame, and being extinguished at the surface is the condition that starts it.

## Direction

Downward and outward through [the moor](place_das_moor.md), one layer at a time, at a rate measured in metres per month rather than per hour. It follows the organic seam wherever it runs, under paths, under roads, under firebreaks, since none of those are cut deep enough to be a boundary for it.

## Lever

The carrying force is retained heat in a bed that holds it; the resistance is moisture and the limited oxygen that reaches depth. Neither side wins outright, which is the point: the burn survives precisely because it is slow enough never to exhaust its own air.

## Echo

The ground above is intact and the ground below is gone. Roots are ash while the tree is still standing, and the surface reports nothing until it collapses or [the entity](persona_das_wesen.md) comes back up through it.
