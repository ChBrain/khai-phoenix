# Plot: Der Abstieg

## Taxonomy

[Moorleiche](play_moorleiche.md), its second movement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

Below the surface of [the moor](place_das_moor.md): the wet corpses of ancient forests in layers, mouldering sinews of dead spruce, iron and bone in the taste of it. No light reaches here and none is coming. It is quiet in a way the production has not been quiet before.

## Action

[The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) rebuilds itself for the dark and runs [its own scheme](plan_ueberwintern.md): it scratches out its eyes because light is no longer useful, reduces its intake to what the air at depth permits, and begins to eat crumb by crumb, layer by layer, where it used to swallow whole. [The smouldering](process_schwelen.md) settles into its rate. Nothing opposes it and nothing helps it.

## Stage

The entity alone, at depth, with no other figure present. Above it, out of reach and out of hearing, the sector stands empty with the board at its edge.

## Tension

It narrates the descent as an acceptance and it is nothing of the kind. Every sentence it says about not needing light is built against the memory of the wall of gold, and it cannot stop measuring the new size against the old one. That unspoken grief is what makes it patient, and patience is the only capability its type has. The Action would be a simple reduction if the entity were what it claims to be; it is a survival because it is not.
