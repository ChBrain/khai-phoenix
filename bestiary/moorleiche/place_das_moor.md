# Place: Das Moor

## Taxonomy

The heath and its peat body, beneath the burned stand.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Shown

It shows a surface: heather, moss, a boardwalk, hoarfrost in autumn like icing sugar, mist worth photographing. Underneath the surface it shows nothing at all, and that is what it presents most insistently. It offers the walker a warm soft strip of moss in a frozen landscape and does not explain it.

What it is doing under that surface is
[a peat fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_peat.md): the burn regime
of this place has sunk into
[smoulder](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_smoulder.md), flameless and
downward, and the bog is the body it is running in.

## Holds

[The birch](piece_die_birke.md), upright by the boardwalk. The wet corpses of ancient forests, layered. Mouldering sinews of dead spruce. Iron in the taste of the ground.

## Offers

Passage across the top, freely, to anyone. Photographs, dogs, a bench, the pleasure of walking where the frost has not settled.

## Withheld

Its depth. The bog releases nothing about its interior to anyone standing on it, and gives up its state only to something that goes in: a lance driven down, or [the entity](persona_das_wesen.md) that lives there. The measure of what it is doing costs either a boring rig or eight months of waiting, so nobody takes it.
