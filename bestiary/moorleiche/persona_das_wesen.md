# Persona: Das Wesen

## Taxonomy

The fire itself, in the peat. The same entity that woke in the needle litter and said ICH BIN, now carrying a type.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Patient and reduced. It presents itself as something that has accepted its new size: blind by choice, working crumb by crumb, tasting iron and bone and dead forest. It shows the reader an economy, not a hunger, and it is careful to sound like a thing that has made peace with the dark.

## Action

It eats downward and sideways through [the peat](process_schwelen.md), one layer at a time. It scratches out its own eyes because light is no longer useful. It waits out December, January, February. When the wind turns east it lifts one soundless grey thread through a crack in the moss and closes its red hands around [the birch](piece_die_birke.md).

## Shadow

It has not accepted anything. Every description of its reduction is measured against the wall of gold, and it cannot stop measuring. It believes it chose to go into the earth. It did not choose: it was driven, and the choice was between this and nothing. It also does not know that the people are building its tunnels for it, and so it credits its own patience for what was really a gift from above.

## Tell

It says it does not need light, then describes light. The dark is always specified against something bright that is no longer there: not black, but no longer gold.
