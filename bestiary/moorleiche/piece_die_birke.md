# Piece: Die Birke

## Taxonomy

The standing trees along the boardwalk, of which this is the nearest.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Place

Beside the boardwalk on [the moor](place_das_moor.md), upright in the frost, where the walkers pass it.

## Load Bearing

It is the production's proof and its receipt. A tree standing whole above roots that are already white ash is the whole argument of the play in one object: the surface reports health while the layer below it has been consumed. Remove it and the ending has nothing to close on, and the claim that the ground lies has no witness.

## Apparent

White bark, upright, unremarkable. Rime on the branches. Nothing about it is wrong from any angle available to a person on the boardwalk. It does not lean.

## Yearbook

It seeded on the heath after the last cut and grew where the water table allowed. It stood through the July fire and was still standing when the sector was signed off, which counted in its favour in the survey. Through the autumn it was photographed by walkers. Under it, through those same months, [the smouldering](process_schwelen.md) took its roots one by one. At the end of the production it is the thing the red hands close around, and it has not yet fallen.
