# Position: Die Einsatzleitung

## Taxonomy

The command structure of the fire service, at incident scale.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Has

Authority to declare an incident closed, and the instruments that authority rests on: sector maps, thermal readings, crews, water, [the lances](piece_die_stahllanze.md), and a date that becomes the record.

## Orders

It orders the holder to convert observation into a verdict and to sign it. To decide with what is measurable, on the schedule the deployment allows, and to release the crews when the criteria are met. It orders the holder to be definite in public.

## Loses

It strips the holder of the right to remain unsure. A crew leader may privately suspect the ground; the position cannot record a suspicion, only a status. It also strips the long view: the position closes incidents, so it has no standing to watch a landscape for eight months.

## Drives

It drives the incident to a close, which is the one motion it is built to produce. Everything it does converts an open situation into a finished one, and the finishing is what hands the ground back to [the entity](persona_das_wesen.md) unobserved.
