# Plot: Das Ertrinken

## Taxonomy

[Moorleiche](play_moorleiche.md), its first movement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

[The moor](place_das_moor.md) under a burned stand, four days into an incident. The air is white with steam rather than smoke. The heather is trodden flat along the hose lines, the light is flat, and the noise that has filled the valley for a week is beginning to stop.

## Action

[The crew leader](persona_der_truppfuehrer.md) drives [the suppression](plan_loeschen.md) to its surface conclusion: tons of water from the air, hoses out of the dust, until the crowns are cold and the roar is choked out. [The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) is doing the only thing left available to it, which is going downward, and [the smouldering](process_schwelen.md) begins in the same hour the flame ends. The two vectors collide at the surface and pass through each other without either party noticing.

## Stage

The crews on the fire lines with hoses and the aircraft above them. The board goes up at the edge of the sector with a date written on it: 14 July, fire extinguished. Soot is wiped off cheeks. Someone smiles.

## Tension

The crew leader carries a definition he has never examined: that a fire is a thing which burns where it can be seen. It is not a lie and he could not be argued out of it, because every instrument he has agrees. The entity carries the reciprocal blindness, believing it chose to descend. The Action cannot complete cleanly because the two of them are agreeing, in perfect confidence, about an event that only one of them survived.
