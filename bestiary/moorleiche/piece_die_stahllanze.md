# Piece: Die Stahllanze

## Taxonomy

The ground-fire kit: lances, pumps, and the hoses that feed them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Place

Driven into [the moor](place_das_moor.md) in a grid across the treated sector, then pulled and moved on. Between deployments it rides on the vehicles with the crews.

## Load Bearing

It is the instrument through which the production's paradox operates. Remove it and the suppression is merely insufficient; with it, the suppression is the fire's infrastructure. Without the lance, [the aeration](process_belueftung.md) has no cause and the Arc has no turn: the fire would still survive, but nobody would have helped it.

## Apparent

A steel tube, two metres, driven by hand or by hammer. Hundred-degree steam hisses out of the puncture where it enters. The ground around it darkens with wet. It looks exactly like a treatment being administered, which is what everyone present sees.

## Yearbook

Bought for peat work after a dry decade made it necessary. Used through the July operation across the whole northern sector, a shaft every two metres, three passes. Pulled in August, cleaned, racked. In September it was listed in the incident report as the reason the ground could be declared safe. It has never been used anywhere its effect could be observed afterwards.
