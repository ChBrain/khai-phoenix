# Plot: Das Mahlen

## Taxonomy

[Erdbäcker](play_erdbaecker.md), its second movement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

The seam, working. Drizzle above, water standing in puddles on [the forest floor](place_der_waldboden.md), and the smell of wet moss that nobody underground can reach.

## Action

[The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) takes the dust, drinks the wax traces of old pine bark, melts them in the dark, and drives the vapour upward. [The sealing](process_versiegeln.md) runs: pore by pore, millimetre by millimetre, the layer over its back is closed. It spends heat at the surface in order to have none there.

## Stage

The entity and the vapour, working upward into cold soil. [The crust](piece_die_kruste.md) forms above it, three metres off the path.

## Tension

It is building the instrument of its own concealment and calls it baking, a domestic word for a defensive act. What it will not say is that it is not hiding from the crews, who have already gone: it is hiding from the possibility of being looked for. The Action cannot resolve because the thing it is defending against has not happened yet and may never, and it has organised its whole existence around it anyway.
