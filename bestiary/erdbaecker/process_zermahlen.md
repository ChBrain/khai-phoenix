# Process: Das Zermahlen

## Taxonomy

[Duff fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_duff.md), the spine of this production: a ground fire smouldering at the very limit of self-sustaining combustion, where mineral soil intermixed through the forest floor decides whether it propagates at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Initiated by

A surface fire sinks into the forest floor and finds fermentation and humus horizons laced with quartz sand and silt, dry enough at depth and just organic enough to carry. The mineral fraction must be low enough to permit it and is high enough to nearly forbid it: the process starts only inside that narrow band.

## Direction

Sideways and slowly through the organic seam, at roughly six hundred degrees held between grains that are not burning, following the needle residue wherever it runs and passing under roads, ditches, and survey stakes without regarding any of them as boundaries.

## Lever

The force is retained heat in the organic fraction; the resistance is the mineral fraction acting as an inert heat sink, drawing warmth out of the reaction into sand that gives nothing back. Neither wins. The whole production lives in that stalemate: too much sand and it dies, too little and it would be [a peat fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_peat.md) instead, robust and self-sustaining and nothing like this.

## Echo

Pebbles warmed and left cooled in white dead powder, the organic layer gone from under an intact surface, and a floor that is now a lid over a void.
