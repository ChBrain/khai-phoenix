# Process: Das Versiegeln

## Taxonomy

A step of [the grinding](process_zermahlen.md): what the burn does to the ground directly above it, and the technique the entity has made out of its own weakness.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Initiated by

Wax traces in old pine bark are melted at depth and the vapour is driven upward into a cool compacted layer. Wherever vapour meets cold soil, the process is already running.

## Direction

Upward into the pores, millimetre by millimetre, until the layer above the burn is closed. It moves toward a surface that transmits neither smoke nor heat.

## Lever

The force is vapour pressure from below; the resistance is the cold blanket of quartz sand, silt, and matted needle residue pressing down with the dead weight of the world. The resistance is not defeated, it is recruited: the vapour condenses into it and makes it tighter than it was.

## Echo

[A crust](piece_die_kruste.md) where there was porous ground. Above it, four degrees and no smoke. Below it, the burn, with its own air supply seeping through what pores remain.
