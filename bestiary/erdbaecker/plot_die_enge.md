# Plot: Die Enge

## Taxonomy

[Erdbäcker](play_erdbaecker.md), its first movement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

Five centimetres under the moss of [the forest floor](place_der_waldboden.md): quartz sand, silt, matted needle residue pressed into a tough cool blanket, and the dead weight of the world on top of it. No room, no wind, no sky. The temperature is six hundred degrees and it is wedged between two grains that are cold.

## Action

[The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) takes stock of what it now is by naming what it does not have, and [the grinding](process_zermahlen.md) settles into the only rate the mineral fraction permits. There is no counter-party in this movement: the opposition is the sand itself, drawing heat out of the reaction and giving nothing back.

## Stage

The entity alone in the seam, with the sand, the silt, and the wax traces in old pine bark. Above it, out of reach, the wet surface and the slack hoses.

## Tension

It presents the narrows as a condition it is working inside, and treats the margin as its own achievement. The margin is not its own: one wetter spring and the process simply stops, technique or no technique. It is carrying a belief in its own agency that the mechanism does not support, and the whole production runs on that gap.
