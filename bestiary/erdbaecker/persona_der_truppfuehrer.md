# Persona: Der Truppführer

## Taxonomy

The incident command at its working end, named here in prose: the man who closes the manifold and stands the crew down.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Reasonable and nearly done. Drizzle on his forehead, helmet pushed back, hoses slack in wet moss and water standing in puddles on the forest floor. He presents the sector as finished and he has the readings to support it.

## Action

He runs [the stand-down](plan_abschluss.md): reads [the drone](piece_die_drohne.md) report, kicks a damp clod of humus with his heel, finds it cold, mouldering, wet, and gives the order to close the manifold and pack up.

## Shadow

He tests with his body and believes the result. The heel is his real instrument, and the heel is exactly what [the crust](piece_die_kruste.md) is built to satisfy. He has no model in which a surface can be manufactured by the thing hiding under it, so a firm cold floor reads to him as evidence rather than as work product.

## Tell

He verifies by pressing down. Every check he makes is a downward force applied to a surface, and he never once asks what the surface is resting on.
