# Pitch: Die Kalte Stimme

## Taxonomy

The key of [Erdbäcker](play_erdbaecker.md), tuned one step further down than the beast before it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Tenor

Mineral reportage. Pressure, grain, millimetres, and heat that does not glow. The entity has no light to describe and no size to mourn, so the register is closer to a soil survey than to a voice: it states depths and temperatures and what it does to the pores above it.

## Undertow

Something like pride, admitted nowhere. It is the most marginal fire in the house and it has turned that into technique, and the flat delivery is the delivery of a thing that knows it is getting away with something.

## Nerve

The gap between an accurate instrument and a true conclusion. It plays for the reader's trust in measurement, and then puts the ember ten centimetres under the boot that just tested the ground.

## Echo

Doubt about a floor. The reader should not be able to read the phrase solid ground the same way afterwards.
