# Plot: Der Freispruch

## Taxonomy

[Erdbäcker](play_erdbaecker.md), its third movement, where the stake is won by being measured as lost.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

[The forest floor](place_der_waldboden.md) washed and quiet, drizzle coming down, hoses slack in the moss, the operation winding up. The kind of afternoon on which things get signed.

## Action

[The crew leader](persona_der_truppfuehrer.md) executes [the stand-down](plan_abschluss.md): the grid is flown, the reading comes back four degrees throughout with no heat nests and no smoke, and he verifies it with his heel against a damp clod of humus. Cold, mouldering, wet. He orders the manifold closed. [The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) does nothing at all in this movement, which is its entire contribution: [the crust](piece_die_kruste.md) it built in the last movement does the work of answering every question that is asked.

## Stage

[The drone](piece_die_drohne.md) over the grid, its report read aloud on the radio. The crew leader with his helmet pushed back, drizzle on his forehead, boot heel into the humus. Ten centimetres under that boot, the ember moving through the next fragment of needle.

## Tension

Every reading in this scene is accurate and the conclusion drawn from all of them is false. The crew leader carries a method that cannot distinguish a cold floor from a manufactured one, and no result it returns will ever tell him which he has. The plot cannot close cleanly because the closure is the error: signing it off is what hands the ground back.
