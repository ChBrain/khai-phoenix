# Plot: Der Schritt

## Taxonomy

[Erdbäcker](play_erdbaecker.md), its fourth movement and its close.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

The washed forest, later. Heather silvering in the mist on [the forest floor](place_der_waldboden.md), the path springing wet like a soaked sponge, nobody working the sector any more.

## Action

[The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) moves: under the forest roads, under the ditches, under the white measuring stakes, warming pebbles and leaving them cooled in white dead powder. [The witness](persona_die_zeugin.md) walks the path three metres from [the crust](piece_die_kruste.md), where raindrops bead like mercury and vanish hissing into nothing. Their trajectories do not collide until the last line, and then they do.

## Stage

The woman, the waxed jacket, the collar pulled higher. The path. The grey crust beside it. The pebbles, warmed and left. Her foot coming down.

## Tension

She has seen the one unambiguous thing in the entire production, rain boiling on contact in a wet forest, and she has no category that would let it mean anything. The knowledge is available and unusable, which is the exact condition everyone in the play has been in. The Action ends where it does because there is nothing left to withhold: the ground gives way, and the only figure who was never told there was a contest is the one who settles it.
