# Play: Erdbäcker

## Estate

[khai-phoenix](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-phoenix), the Phoenix: the bestiary
of fire that holds this run and answers for it.

Its spine is [duff fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_duff.md), the
wildland phenomenon of a ground fire smouldering at the very limit of
self-sustaining combustion, where the mineral fraction intermixed through the
forest floor acts as an inert heat sink and decides whether it propagates at all.
It leans on [smoulder](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_smoulder.md) from the
regime, and on [peat](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_peat.md) as its
contrast: peat's burn is robust and self-sustaining, this one is not, and the
whole production lives in that difference.

## Name

**Erdbäcker.** The earth baker: something under the ground working slow heat into
the soil above it until the pores close. The name is billed over a production
about a fire whose only remaining ambition is to be measured as cold.

## Arc

To become undetectable it must give up being detectable, and heat at the surface
is what it gives up.

The bend is that its weakness is its concealment. This is the most marginal fire
in the house: six hundred degrees wedged between two cold grains of quartz sand,
propagating only because the numbers happen to permit it, and one wet spring away
from simply stopping. Any other beast would call that dying. This one converts it
into method. It bakes the pores of the earth shut above its own back, and the
crust it builds is what the instruments read.

So the drone flies, the thermal camera reports four degrees of ground temperature
across the whole sector, the crew leader kicks a wet clod of humus with his heel
and finds it cold, modrig, nass. Every measurement is accurate. The conclusion is
wrong. Ten centimetres below the boot the ember is moving through the next needle
fragment, and it goes under the forest roads, under their ditches, under their
white measuring stakes.

Reordered, this is a fire hiding. In order, it is a fire that made being nearly
dead into the technique by which it is not.

## Triggers

Four plots, each closing on the next one's cue.

1. **Die Enge.** It takes stock of what it now is: no room for wings, no wind out
   of France, no sky full of gold. Quartz sand and silt, six hundred degrees held
   between two cold grains. Cue: the dead weight of the world settles on it.
   Closes when it starts working rather than mourning, which fires:
2. **Das Mahlen.** It takes the dust, drinks the wax traces of old pine bark,
   melts them in the dark, drives the vapour upward, and bakes the pores shut
   millimetre by millimetre. Closes when the crust is sealed, which fires:
3. **Der Freispruch.** The drone reports sector C entirely unremarkable, four
   degrees throughout, no heat nests, no smoke. The heel finds cold wet humus. The
   manifold is closed. Every reading is correct. Closes on the sentence: hier
   brennt nichts mehr, which fires:
4. **Der Schritt.** It moves under the forest roads, under the ditches, under the
   white stakes, warming pebbles and leaving them cooled in white dead powder.
   Closes on a foot coming down and the ground giving way.

## Stakes

**Fortdauer:** whether this fire continues as itself, where continuing means
being read as absent.

The contest is quieter than the one before it because one side does not know it
is in a contest. The people are not fighting this fire, they are concluding it,
and their conclusion is drawn from instruments that are working properly. The
entity is not fighting them either. It is baking a lid.

Every plot moves it. Die Enge sets the margin it has to live inside, which is
almost none. Das Mahlen builds the crust, spending heat at the surface to buy
invisibility. Der Freispruch is the stake being won by being measured as lost:
the sign-off is the victory. Der Schritt collects, and it collects on the one
party who was never told there was a contest, which is why the production ends on
a walker's foot rather than on the fire.
