# Piece: Die Kruste

## Taxonomy

The surface layer of [the forest floor](place_der_waldboden.md), where the burn has closed it.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Place

Three metres off the path on [the forest floor](place_der_waldboden.md), over the
seam wherever the burn has run: it is not one patch but a moving lid, and it is
always directly above [the entity](persona_das_wesen.md).

## Load Bearing

It is the interface the entire production runs through, and the only object in the play that lies to everyone at once. Remove it and the drone sees heat, the heel goes through, and there is no play: the fire would simply be found. With it, every instrument is accurate and every conclusion is wrong. It is also, literally, load bearing: it carries a boot and then stops carrying one.

## Apparent

Grey, closed, unremarkable. Raindrops bead on it like mercury and vanish hissing. It looks like ground that has been rained on, and from three metres away on a wet afternoon it looks like every other part of the sector.

## Yearbook

It did not exist in July. It was made through the autumn, millimetre by millimetre, by [the sealing](process_versiegeln.md) driving wax vapour up into cold soil. In the drone survey it returned four degrees along with everything around it, and it was on the strength of that reading, among others, that the sector was called unremarkable. It took the crew leader's heel without giving a centimetre. It has been photographed once, incidentally, by a walker who was photographing the mist. At the end of the production it takes one more step and does not hold.
