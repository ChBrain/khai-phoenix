# Plan: Der Abschluss

## Taxonomy

A mandate: the closing scheme the fireground is directed at, run to release a sector and its crews.

## Owner

- Owner: [Der Truppführer](persona_der_truppfuehrer.md)

## Direction

Release the sector on defensible evidence and go home. The crews have been out for days, the rain has arrived, and every reading supports closure. The plan is the correct plan, arrived at correctly, and it is aimed five centimetres above the fire.

## Orders

- To [the drone](piece_die_drohne.md) operator: fly the confirmation grid over sector C and report ground temperature, heat nests, and smoke.
- To the crew leader: verify on foot, by spot check.
- To the crews: close the manifold, pack up.

## Implementation

By instrument first and body second: thermal survey from above, then a heel into a damp clod of humus. Both tests read the surface, because the surface is the only thing either test can reach, and nothing in the plan calls for breaking it.
