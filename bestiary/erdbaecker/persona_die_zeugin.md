# Persona: Die Zeugin

## Taxonomy

The public on the path: a woman walking the washed forest after the rain.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Composed and incurious. She pulls the collar of her waxed jacket higher against the damp and reads the landscape as weather: heather silvering in the mist, a path springing wet underfoot like a soaked sponge.

## Action

She walks [the forest floor](place_der_waldboden.md), three metres from a grey crust where raindrops bead like mercury and vanish hissing into nothing. She does not stop. At the end of the production her foot comes down.

## Shadow

She is the only figure with a direct, unmediated observation of the fire, and she has no category for it. Rain that boils on contact three metres away is not weather, and some part of her registers it as wrong without the thought completing. She trusts a forest path the way she trusts a floor, which is to say she has never once considered it.

## Tell

Her attention slides off the one thing in frame that is not behaving. She notices the mercury and keeps walking.
