# Persona: Das Wesen

## Taxonomy

The fire itself, in the duff. The same entity, two types down from the birth, with the least of everything it has ever had.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Purely functional. It presents no light, no flame, and no nostalgia: it opens by saying what it does not do, then says what it does, which is grind. It shows the reader an operation running at its lower limit and treats that as unremarkable.

## Action

It takes the dust. It drinks the wax traces of old pine bark, melts them in the dark, and drives the vapour upward. It [bakes the pores](process_versiegeln.md) of the earth shut over its own back, millimetre by millimetre, and then moves under the forest roads, under the ditches, under the white measuring stakes.

## Shadow

It has organised its entire existence around not being perceived, and it never asks why that should be the goal. Being read as absent is not survival to it, it is the achievement, and the production is the record of a thing that has confused invisibility with living. It also cannot see that its margin is externally set: one wet spring ends it regardless of technique.

## Tell

It measures everything and compares nothing. Six hundred degrees, five centimetres, ten centimetres, millimetre by millimetre, and never once a rate of progress or a destination. The numbers are all position and no direction, which is what a thing sounds like when it has stopped going anywhere.
