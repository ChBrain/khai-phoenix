# Place: Der Waldboden

## Taxonomy

The burned sector after the rain, from the moss down to the mineral soil.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Shown

It shows a washed forest: heather silvering in the mist, a path springing wet underfoot like a soaked sponge, water standing in puddles, hoses lying slack in wet moss. Five centimetres down it shows nothing at all, and to any instrument it shows four degrees.

What it is doing under that surface is
[a duff fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_duff.md): the burn regime
of this place has sunk into
[smoulder](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_smoulder.md), flameless and
marginal, running at the edge of what will carry at all.

## Holds

Quartz sand, silt, matted needle residue pressed into a tough cool blanket. Damp clods of humus. Wax traces in old pine bark. [The crust](piece_die_kruste.md), three metres off the path. Forest roads, ditches, and white measuring stakes.

## Offers

Walking, freely, to anyone: it takes weight without complaint and springs back wet. It offers a heel-test to whoever wants one and returns cold, mouldering, and firm.

## Withheld

Its own state. The floor releases what it is doing only to something that goes into it deeper than a boot or a thermal camera can reach, and it charges for that in the only currency it accepts: a step that goes through.
