# Piece: Die Drohne

## Taxonomy

The survey kit: airframe, thermal camera, and the radio link back to command.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Place

Above the sector at survey height, flown in a grid; between sorties, in its case in the vehicle.

## Load Bearing

It is what converts the entity's concealment into an official fact. Remove it and the sector is closed on a man's judgement, which could be doubted; with it, the closure rests on an instrument reading, which cannot. It supplies the production's central irony: a device that measures perfectly and therefore certifies a falsehood.

## Apparent

A small airframe with a gimballed thermal camera, working properly. Its output is a clean uniform field: four degrees of ground temperature across the whole of sector C, no heat nests, no smoke development. The picture is correct.

## Yearbook

Brought in after the main operation for the confirmation sweep, when crews were being released and the sector needed a defensible sign-off. It flew the grid twice in drizzle. Its report was read aloud on the radio, entered in the log, and used as the basis for closing the manifold. Nobody has flown it over the same ground since, and if they did, in the same conditions, it would return the same reading.
