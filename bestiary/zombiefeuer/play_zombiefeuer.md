# Play: Zombiefeuer

## Estate

[khai-phoenix](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-phoenix), the Phoenix: the bestiary
of fire that holds this run and answers for it.

Its spine is
[holdover fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_holdover.md), and the
member is explicit about what kind of thing it is: not a distinct combustion but
a temporal one, the subsurface smoulder read across time, the fire that outlasts
the season that lit it. Where the two beasts before it are named for a substrate,
this one is named for a duration.

It leans on [smoulder](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_smoulder.md) from the
regime, and on [peat](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_peat.md) and
[duff](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_duff.md) as the beds it is lying
in: the thermal reservoir the earlier productions drove downward is what it now
has to live off for eight months.

## Name

**Zombiefeuer**, and the creature is **der Frostschläfer**, the frost sleeper.
The name in the trade is the honest one: a fire that is dead by every record and
gets up anyway.

## Arc

It survives the winter, and the surviving costs it its name.

The bend is that the cost is not paid in the cold. Crossing the winter is only
hard: it goes dormant below the frost, lives on retained heat, and does nothing
at all for months, which is the one thing its type is built for. The price comes
in the thaw. When the surface dries and air reaches it, it stands back up as an
ordinary surface fire, and ordinary is exactly what damns it: it looks like a
beginning. It is written down as the first fire of the new season, given a new
number, and credited to a spark that never happened.

So the record contains no fire that survived a winter. It contains one that ended
in autumn and another that started in spring, and the two are the same animal.
The entity wins the only fight that was on offer and loses the one it did not
know it was in.

Reordered, this is a fire that waited. In order, it is a fire that came back and
was not recognised.

## Company

The closed cast. A production draws from here and no further.

- **das Wesen**, the fire, speaking: dormant, patient, and reduced to retained
  heat. It has crossed a winter, which nothing else in the house has done.
- **der Truppführer**, the one who closes the file in autumn: competent,
  correct, and finished. His competence stops being relevant the moment the
  mandate ends, which is the point of him.
- **die Brandstatistik**, the office that counts fires and dates them: it decides
  what is a new fire and what is a continuation, and it has no category for the
  second.
- **die Schneedecke**, the place: the snow layer over the burned ground, which
  insulates the thing it hides and reports it in one place only.
- **der Schmelzring**, the piece: the ring of thawed snow over warm ground. The
  member calls the melt rings diagnostic, so the sign is legible: it is simply
  never in front of anyone. It is the production's one piece of evidence and it
  is unobserved, not misread.
- **die Brandakte**, the piece: the file, closed in autumn and opened again in
  spring under a different number.
- **das Überdauern**, the process: living on retained heat below the frost, doing
  nothing, for as long as it takes.
- **die Neueröffnung**, the process: how a continuation becomes a beginning in
  the record.
- **Winterruhe**, the plan: the closed season, run by the office. Nobody monitors
  the deep layer, because monitoring the deep layer through a winter is the one
  thing that would end it.
- **die Kalte Stimme**, the pitch: the default key, tuned slower. It reports.

Files for these land with the board; here they are named, not linked.

## Triggers

Four plots, each closing on the next one's cue.

1. **Das Zudecken.** The season ends. Snow closes over the ground, the file is
   closed, the crews are gone, and the entity goes below the frost. Cue: the
   first snow that stays. Closes when the file is closed, which fires:
2. **Die Dunkelziffer.** The winter, at length: December, January, February,
   March. Nothing happens, and the nothing is the movement. No human being
   appears in it at all: the ground is frozen and under snow, the file is closed,
   and nobody is assigned. Its one sign, a ring of thawed snow over warm ground,
   forms and refreezes and forms again with no one on the ground to see it.
   Closes when the surface begins to dry, which fires:
3. **Das Aufstehen.** Air reaches it, the fuels cure, and it stands back up into
   flame for the first time since autumn, behaving exactly like any surface fire
   that has just started. Closes when it is seen and reported, which fires:
4. **Der fremde Name.** It is entered as the first fire of the year, under a new
   number, attributed to a cause that did not occur. Closes on the record being
   correct in every field and wrong in the only one that matters.

## Stakes

**Der Name:** whether what comes back is recorded as itself.

Continuation is not in doubt in this production, which is what separates it from
the two before it. Nobody is fighting the fire over the winter, because nobody
knows there is a fire; the deep layer is never monitored and the entity is never
in danger. What is contested is identity, and the contest is one-sided: the
entity has no way to make a claim, and the office has no field to record one in.

Every plot moves it. Das Zudecken closes the record while the thing it describes
is still alive, and ends the mandate that would have kept anyone looking. Die
Dunkelziffer is the stake held in suspension for four months, with one legible
piece of evidence on the ground and nobody present: the failure is absence, not
error, which is what separates this production from the two before it. Das
Aufstehen returns the entity to visibility in the worst possible form, looking
exactly like something new. Der fremde Name settles it on paper, and the paper is
what survives.
