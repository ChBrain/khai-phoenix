# Play: Ich Bin

## Estate

[khai-phoenix](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-phoenix), the Phoenix: the bestiary
of fire that holds this run and answers for it. Every production staged here is
one fire speaking itself, and the house's declared repertoire is what it may
speak out of: the [combustion](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_combustion.md)
engine for the named phenomena, the [fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_fire.md)
engine for the regime beneath them, and the
[blaze](@chbrain/khai-composite-blaze/process_blaze.md) composite where a fire
lives a whole life.

## Name

**Ich Bin.** The name comes from the end of the birth: a fire that has eaten,
run, and grown until there is something there to say _I_. Every production billed
under this name begins after that sentence and asks the question it opens: _I am,
but what am I?_ The prologue (Zunderkind) is not staged here. It is the backstory
every run inherits, and the reason the entity already has a self to lose.

## Arc

The fire wakes as a self before it is a kind of fire. To go on existing it must
take a form, and the form that lets it live is the same one that cages it: one
way of eating, one weakness, one ending. What it gains in being something, it
loses in being anything.

The bend is the price. In the first movement the type is a gift, the only way to
keep burning. In the last it is a sentence: the peat that survived the water can
never again be the wall of gold, and the duff that made itself invisible had to
give up its heat at the surface to do it. The world attacks the form, and the
form answers with its own paradox, so the attack feeds it or misses it entirely.
And what the fire is left with at the end is exactly what its type permits it to
be left with: persistence, re-emergence, or nothing.

Reordered, this is a catalogue. In order, it is the cost of becoming specific.

## Company

The closed cast. A production draws from here and no further.

**The variable, called at staging:**

- [the type](process_typ.md): the form the fire takes in this run. Its spine is
  always a member of the combustion engine, never fire and never blaze. A run may
  carry more than one type at once, or move between them: one fire, several
  forms. Uncalled by default, so the Director may name the type, name a set, or
  leave it uncalled and read what the cast reaches for.

**Personas:**

- [das Wesen](persona_das_wesen.md): the fire, speaking. It is one entity across
  every production and every type, and it remembers having been the other thing.
- [der Wind](persona_der_wind.md): the second element, a partner and never a
  servant. Present when the type can use him, absent when it cannot, and his
  absence is a statement.
- [der Truppführer](persona_der_truppfuehrer.md): the one who works against it,
  by procedure, in the flat speech of the job.
- [die Zeugin](persona_die_zeugin.md): the one who lives on the ground it burns
  in, and mostly does not know.

**Positions:**

- [die Einsatzleitung](position_einsatzleitung.md): the office that decides the
  fire is out, and signs it.

**Processes:**

- [das Fressen](process_fressen.md): the type's own way of taking fuel, which is
  the entity's only real action.
- [die Kehrseite](process_kehrseite.md): the paradox the type carries, where the
  obvious way to fight it fails or feeds it.

**Places:**

- [der Grund](place_der_grund.md): the ground this type burns in, which the type
  chooses: the forest floor, the room, the tank, the mound.

**Pieces:**

- [die Kruste](piece_die_kruste.md): the surface between the fire and the world,
  which reports the fire wrongly.

**Plans:**

- [Löschen](plan_loeschen.md): the suppression scheme, run by the Einsatzleitung.
  Its steps are the world's, and its targets are where the Kehrseite bites.

**Pitch:**

- [die Kalte Stimme](pitch_die_kalte_stimme.md): the default key. The entity is
  neither monster nor victim. It reports.

## Triggers

Four plots, each closing on the next one's cue.

1. **Das Erwachen.** The entity comes to in a form it did not choose, and reads
   what it now is by what it can and cannot reach. Cue: the fire has a self and
   no kind. Closes when the type is fixed, which fires:
2. **Das Fressen.** The type eats in its own way, at its own rate, in its own
   ground. This is the run at full extension and the only movement where the
   entity is simply itself. Closes when the world notices, which fires:
3. **Der Zugriff.** The world moves against the form by procedure, and the
   Kehrseite turns: the water aerates, the instrument reads cold, the barrier is
   the thing that carries it. Closes when the attack is spent and the record says
   the fire is out, which fires:
4. **Der Verbleib.** What the type permits to remain. Persistence, re-emergence,
   or nothing, and the entity knows which of the three its form allows.

## Stakes

**Fortdauer:** whether this fire continues as itself.

Three parties contest it and none of them can settle it alone. The world wants it
stopped and has procedures. The entity wants to go on and has only its mechanism.
The type stands between them and decides what continuing would even mean: for
some forms it means overwintering underground for eight months, for others it
means the next four minutes and then nothing at all.

Every plot moves it. Das Erwachen sets what continuance can look like at all. Das
Fressen spends the fuel that buys it. Der Zugriff is the world's attempt to end
it, which the Kehrseite may convert into the thing that extends it. Der Verbleib
settles it, and the settlement is a verdict on the form, not on the fire.
