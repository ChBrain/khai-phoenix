# Plot: Die bloße Hand

## Taxonomy

[Frostschläfer](play_frostschlaefer.md), its second movement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

The same slope on an ordinary afternoon of the closed season. Frost air, silver fir, tracks in the powder, and a man walking his own ground with no particular reason to look down.

## Action

[Martin](persona_martin.md) stops when the white breaks. He goes down on his knees at [the black eye](piece_das_schwarze_auge.md), takes his glove off with his teeth, presses a bare palm flat into the mud, digs his fingers in, and lifts them to his nose. Then he calls it in: no rot, sweet smouldering, sour wood, heat on the pads, and send the crew with the long earth lances.

## Stage

The hand-sized hole of black peat in flawless snow, a hair-thin thread of vapour standing out of it. A glove in his teeth, a radio at his mouth, larches downslope that he is thinking about the whole time.

## Tension

He is right about everything he can touch and has not asked what the remedy does. The diagnosis is complete and the prescription is reflexive: lances, because that is what ground fire takes. Nothing in his experience would prompt the question, and the plot cannot close cleanly because the best work in the production is what sets the rest of it moving.
