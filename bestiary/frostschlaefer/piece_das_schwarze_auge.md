# Piece: Das schwarze Auge

## Taxonomy

The melt hole in [the snow cover](piece_die_schneedecke.md), which the trade calls a melt ring and treats as diagnostic.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Place

In the middle of the flawless white of [the plantation](place_die_schonung.md), on the slope beside the new planting, roughly where the burn lies ten centimetres under the moss.

## Load Bearing

It is the entire chain of causation in one object the size of a hand. Remove it and nobody finds the fire, nobody calls for the lances, the frost is never opened, and the entity waits for the thaw like any other holdover. Everything that happens in this production happens because a fire that was hiding perfectly could not hide the one thing it exhales.

## Apparent

Bare black peat, barely hand-sized, in unbroken snow. A hair-thin thread of warm vapour standing up out of it and breaking in the frost air. Wet mud in the middle of a frozen slope, and nothing else about the hillside is wrong.

## Yearbook

It opened at some point in the winter and has been reopening ever since, freezing over at night and thawing again by day, unattended and unrecorded. Deer walked around it. On one afternoon in the closed season a man knelt at it, put a bare hand into it, smelled his fingers, and read it correctly on the first attempt. He scratched its edge, and that scratch is the crack the entity retreated from.
