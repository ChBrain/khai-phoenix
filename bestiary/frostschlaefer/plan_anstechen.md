# Plan: Anstechen

## Taxonomy

A mandate: the attack scheme the fire crew is directed at, for a ground fire that cannot be reached from the surface.

## Owner

- Owner: [Die Löschgruppe](persona_die_loeschgruppe.md)

## Direction

Reach the fire where it actually is and drown it there. Surface water is useless against a burn a metre down in root and mycelium, so the water has to be put into the layer itself, and it has to be done before the wind turns. The reasoning is correct at every step.

## Orders

- To the crew: set manifold one, couple the rod sections, bring [the lances](piece_die_erdlanze.md) forward.
- To the crew: pierce to one metre of depth, ten bar on the lances, flood the mycelium.
- To the drone operator: give a surface reading.
- To the forester: show us where.

## Implementation

On [the plantation](place_die_schonung.md) under two feet of snow, in sector Delta-Four beside the new planting. The plan requires the frost layer to be opened in order to work, which is the only way of reaching the fire and also the only thing currently holding it.
