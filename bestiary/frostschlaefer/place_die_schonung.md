# Place: Die Schonung

## Taxonomy

A young plantation on a slope, sector Delta-Four, beside the new planting.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Shown

It shows two feet of frozen crystal over everything, flawless, with three fresh deer tracks crossing the slope and the tiny prints of a dormouse in the powder beside them. Young silver fir, frosted needles, larches further down. It shows a landscape in good order and in the closed season.

What it carries underneath is [smoulder](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_smoulder.md): the regime here has sunk below the frost and is burning downward and unseen, and the surface it presents is the reason nobody is looking.

## Holds

[The snow cover](piece_die_schneedecke.md). [The black eye](piece_das_schwarze_auge.md), barely the size of a hand, in the middle of the white. Deer tracks. Dormouse prints. Root channels, mycelium, wet humus under the moss layer.

## Offers

Walking, tracking, and the ordinary work of a closed season: a forester may cross it, read it, put a hand on any tree, kneel anywhere he likes. It hides nothing from anyone who touches it.

## Withheld

Depth. It gives up the surface freely and the metre below it not at all, and it releases that only to steel driven down, or to a spring thaw. The one sign it does offer is small enough to be missed by anybody who is not looking at the ground.
