# Plot: Der weiße Sarg

## Taxonomy

[Frostschläfer](play_frostschlaefer.md), its first movement.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

[The plantation](place_die_schonung.md) in the closed season: two feet of frozen crystal over everything, no light, no wind, deer and dormouse tracks across a flawless slope.

## Action

[The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) runs [the rationing](process_das_sparen.md) and nothing else: a pine root a month, half a leaf a week, a pulse every three hours, its own foul breath caught in the wet humus under the moss. There is no counter-party. The only other actor is the season.

## Stage

[The snow cover](piece_die_schneedecke.md) over the whole slope, unbroken except at one point. The entity below it, in root channels and mycelium, ten centimetres under the moss layer.

## Tension

It calls the thing holding it down a fur coat, and means it. The cover is the only constraint on it and it experiences that constraint as comfort, so the movement cannot resolve into either captivity or safety: the entity is content in a cell it has not recognised as one, and the contentment is what makes the ending inevitable.
