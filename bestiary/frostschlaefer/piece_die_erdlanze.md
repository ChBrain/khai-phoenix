# Piece: Die Erdlanze

## Taxonomy

The ground-fire kit: coupled rod sections, pumps, and the hoses that feed them.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Place

On the vehicles until the callout, then coupled section by section and driven into [the plantation](place_die_schonung.md) to a metre of depth.

## Load Bearing

It is the instrument through which a correct diagnosis becomes the fire's release. Remove it and the crew has no way to reach a burn a metre down, and the frost stays shut: the fire is contained by winter and nothing happens. With it, the treatment is also the opening. The play's whole bend runs through this object.

## Apparent

Rod sections that couple together, long enough to reach a metre and more, fed at ten bar. Steel through crust and frost and root. It looks like the only sensible tool for the job, which it is.

## Yearbook

Requested by name over the radio by a forester who had just diagnosed a smoulder with his hands. Carried up the slope by a crew whose thermal camera was reading blue at the time. It has been used before on peat and duff, and it worked there, where a wet bed can actually be flooded. Here it is about to be driven through the only thing rationing the fire.
