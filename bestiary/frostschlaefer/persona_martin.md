# Persona: Martin

## Taxonomy

The forester of this ground, walking his own plantation in the closed season.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Attentive at the scale of a hand. He is reading tracks, bark and needles as he goes, and his attention is habitual rather than alerted: a palm resting on a young silver fir, a look at frosted needles. He is the only figure in the house who touches the ground on purpose.

## Action

He stops when the white breaks. He pulls his glove off with his teeth, kneels, presses a bare palm flat into the morass and digs his fingers in, then lifts them to his nose and closes his eyes. Then he calls it in, precisely: no rot, a sweet smouldering, sour wood, heat against the pads. He asks for the earth lances by name.

## Shadow

His body finds what the instruments cannot, and he trusts it completely, which is correct and is exactly what defeats him. He never doubts the diagnosis, so he never asks the second question: what the recommended treatment does to a fire sealed under frost. He is thinking about his larches, and the larches are a real reason, and it is not the whole picture.

## Tell

He verifies by touch and smell before he verifies by anything else, and he narrates in that order. Instruments come last in his sentences, and in this production they come last in usefulness too.
