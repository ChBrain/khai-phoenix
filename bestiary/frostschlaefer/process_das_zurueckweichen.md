# Process: Das Zurückweichen

## Taxonomy

A step of [the rationing](process_das_sparen.md): what the entity does when the cover is broken before it is ready.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Initiated by

The roof cracks and cold air drops in as pure oxygen. Everything the fire wants is suddenly available in a small opening, at the wrong moment and in the wrong place.

## Direction

Down and away from the disturbance. The heat is pushed deeper into the cold root channels, carried like a glowing egg, out of reach of whatever made the opening.

## Lever

The force is appetite meeting free oxygen, which produces an immediate twitch; the resistance is the entity's own restraint, which wins. This is the only process in the house where the fire declines something, and it declines because a larger opening is coming.

## Echo

A crack that leads nowhere, a searching finger that finds cooling mud, and a fire further down than it was. The moment reads as escape and is a decision.
