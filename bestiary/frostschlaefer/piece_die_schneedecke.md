# Piece: Die Schneedecke

## Taxonomy

The winter layer over [the plantation](place_die_schonung.md): the white coffin, and the fur coat.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Place

Over the whole slope, two feet of it, from the first snow that stays until the thaw.

## Load Bearing

It is the constraint the entire production rests on and the thing both parties act to remove. It seals the cold out, which the entity is grateful for, and it seals the air out, which is the only reason the fire is rationed to a root a month. Remove it and there is no arithmetic, no restraint, no waiting: the beast simply runs. Every stake in the play is a stake about this object.

## Apparent

Flawless white, crusted enough to rattle chains on, deep enough to bury the moss layer completely. It looks like the end of the fire season. Deer cross it without breaking through.

## Yearbook

It closed over ground that had been signed off in autumn and has kept it out of sight since. Through the winter it has taken tracks, held a crust, and shown nothing at all except at one point the size of a hand. It is what the drone is reading when it reports minus four. By the end of the production it is scheduled to be pierced at one metre by men who need to get past it, and it will melt anyway a few weeks later.
