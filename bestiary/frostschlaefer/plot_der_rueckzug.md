# Plot: Der Rückzug

## Taxonomy

[Frostschläfer](play_frostschlaefer.md), its third movement, and the one where the entity chooses.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

The hole, immediately after. Cold air standing over warm mud, the thread of vapour disturbed, and a finger at the edge of it.

## Action

[Martin](persona_martin.md) scratches the crust, and the roof cracks. Icy air drops in as pure oxygen and whips the entity's chambers. It twitches. Then it runs [the withdrawal](process_das_zurueckweichen.md): down into the cold root channels, heat pushed ahead of it like a glowing egg, away from the searching finger.

## Stage

A man's hand at a small opening. Under it, mud that is cooling by the second. Below that, in channels nobody has mapped, a heat that is moving away rather than toward.

## Tension

This is the first refusal in the house, and it is not restraint. The entity declines a small opening because it can hear a larger one being ordered over the radio, and the sentence it uses to explain itself is a comparison with the child it used to be. The Action would run to its natural end, upward, if the entity were still what the first production made. It is not, and the plot turns on the difference.
