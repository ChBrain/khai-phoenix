# Plot: Der geöffnete Sarg

## Taxonomy

[Frostschläfer](play_frostschlaefer.md), its fourth movement and its close.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Cue

The slope with the vehicles on it: heavy four-wheel diesel at idle, chains rattling in the crust, yellow light cutting through snowed crowns. The closed season has been interrupted.

## Action

[The crew](persona_die_loeschgruppe.md) sets manifold one and asks where the hotspot is, because Drone Three reports minus four at the surface and the thermal camera stays completely blue. [Martin](persona_martin.md) points with a shaking hand: ten centimetres under the moss layer. The crew runs [the piercing](plan_anstechen.md): couple the rods, one metre of depth, ten bar, flood the mycelium before the wind turns. [The entity](persona_das_wesen.md) stops hiding and tells them to go ahead.

## Stage

[The lances](piece_die_erdlanze.md) coming forward section by section. [The snow cover](piece_die_schneedecke.md) about to be pierced. Red toes already closed around the deep rotten veins of the wood.

## Tension

Both parties want the same object removed and each believes the removal ends the other. The crew is not mistaken about where the fire is, or about what a metre of depth and ten bar would do to a wet bed. What they are opening is not a wet bed: it is the frost that has been rationing a fire all winter. The production closes on the entity naming the terms out loud, and nobody on the slope can hear it.
