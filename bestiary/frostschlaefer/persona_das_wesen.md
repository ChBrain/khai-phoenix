# Persona: Das Wesen

## Taxonomy

The fire itself, overwintering under snow. The same entity as the birth, three types on, and the first one to have learned anything.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Rationed and unbothered. It presents its reduction as housekeeping rather than as loss: it does not burn, it sips. It describes the snow as a fur coat rather than a lid, and it is not lying, which is the unsettling part.

## Action

It [spends nothing](process_das_sparen.md): a pine root a month, half a leaf a week, a heartbeat every three hours. When the roof cracks and oxygen comes in like a whip, it [goes down instead of up](process_das_zurueckweichen.md), pushing its heat away from a searching finger like a glowing egg. At the end it invites the steel in.

## Shadow

It has confused being contained with being comfortable. Two feet of frost is the only thing rationing it, and it experiences that constraint as a fur coat, so it spends the whole production grateful to the thing that is holding it down. It also does not notice that its restraint is not virtue: it withdraws because it has something better coming, and it says the second part out loud without hearing the first.

## Tell

It measures itself against the child it used to be. The one moment of self-description in the production is a comparison with the beast in the first production, and it is the only sentence where pride gets in.
