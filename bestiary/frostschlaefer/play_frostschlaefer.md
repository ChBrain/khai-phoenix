# Play: Frostschläfer

## Estate

[khai-phoenix](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-phoenix), the Phoenix: the bestiary
of fire that holds this run and answers for it.

Its spine is
[holdover fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_holdover.md), which the
member defines as not a distinct combustion but a temporal one: the subsurface
smoulder read across time, the fire that outlasts the season that lit it. It
leans on [smoulder](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_smoulder.md) from the
regime and on [peat](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_peat.md) for the bed
it is lying in, the black eye of it showing through the snow.

## Name

**Frostschläfer**, the frost sleeper. The trade calls the phenomenon a zombie
fire, and the trade name is the loud one; this production is billed under the
quiet one, because nothing in it is undead and everything in it is asleep.

## Arc

The frost is the only thing holding it, and being found is what gets the frost
taken off.

The bend runs through the men. Two feet of frozen crystal is a coffin and a fur
coat at once: it locks the cold out, and it also locks the fire in, rationing it
to one pine root a month and a pulse every three hours. Nothing above ground
knows it is there. The drone reads minus four at the surface and the thermal
camera stays completely blue, so by instrument the slope is empty.

Then a man finds it with his hands. He takes his glove off with his teeth,
kneels, presses a bare palm flat into the mud, and smells his fingers, and he is
right: no rot, a sweet smouldering, sour wood, heat against the pads of his hand.
That is the best piece of work anyone does in this production, and its reward is
that he calls for the lances. The correct response to what he has found is to
couple the rods, drive them a metre down, and flood the mycelium at ten bar,
which means opening the frost with steel and putting air into it.

So the entity spends the whole production being contained by winter and ends it
being freed by a rescue. Reordered, this is a fire that was found. In order, it
is a fire whose discovery is the thing that lets it out.

## Triggers

Four plots, each closing on the next one's cue.

1. **Der weiße Sarg.** The entity under two feet of frozen crystal, rationing
   itself: no light, no wind, its own foul breath caught in wet humus. Cue: the
   snow closes and the cold locks out. Closes when a man stops on the slope,
   which fires:
2. **Die bloße Hand.** The forester's eye catches the black hole in the white.
   Glove off with the teeth, knees down, palm flat in the mud, fingers to the
   nose, then the radio: no rot, sweet smouldering, sour wood, heat on the pads.
   Closes on the call for the lances, which fires:
3. **Der Rückzug.** His finger scratches the crust and cracks the roof. Icy air
   drops in as pure oxygen and whips the greedy chambers. The entity twitches,
   and then does the thing the child in the first production could not do: it
   goes down and away rather than up. Closes when the diesel arrives, which
   fires:
4. **Der geöffnete Sarg.** Manifold one is up, the drone reports minus four and
   blue, the forester points, the rods are coupled for a metre of depth at ten
   bar. The entity stops hiding and invites it. Closes on the slope changing
   owner when the coffin melts.

## Stakes

**Die Decke:** whether the seal over it stays shut.

Both sides work to open it and only one of them is right about what follows. The
men open it because a fire that is not reached cannot be flooded, and the reading
is correct: the mycelium is where it lives and a metre is where it has to be
fought. The entity wants it open because frost is the only thing rationing it,
and steel through the frost is air.

Every plot moves it. Der weiße Sarg establishes the seal as the fire's whole
constraint, and its comfort. Die bloße Hand is the seal proving penetrable to a
bare palm when it was opaque to a thermal camera. Der Rückzug is the first crack
and the entity declining to take it, which is what buys it the larger opening.
Der geöffnete Sarg settles it: the seal comes off by agreement between two
parties who each believe they are ending the other.
