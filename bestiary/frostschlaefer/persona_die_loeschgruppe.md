# Persona: Die Löschgruppe

## Taxonomy

The crew that arrives with the gear, at the working end of the callout.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Projection

Loud, equipped and impatient. Heavy diesel at idle, chains in the crust, yellow light through snowed crowns. They present as the moment the situation gets taken seriously, and they announce their own readiness before they have located anything.

## Action

They set the manifold, ask where the hotspot is, read the drone back as minus four with the FLIR completely blue, take the forester's pointed finger instead, then [drive the lances](plan_anstechen.md) to a metre and flood the mycelium at ten bar before the wind turns.

## Shadow

They arrive with the answer already chosen. The question they ask on the ground is where, never what, so the diagnosis they act on is a location rather than a description, and the treatment was decided before anyone got out of the vehicle. Their instruments say nothing is there and they proceed anyway, which is right, and they never notice that this makes the instruments irrelevant rather than wrong.

## Tell

They speak in imperatives about equipment. Every line is a piece of kit and a number, and none of them is about the fire.
