# Process: Das Sparen

## Taxonomy

[Holdover fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_holdover.md), the spine of this production: the fire that overwinters and re-emerges, read here as the budget it keeps to do so.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Initiated by

The surface freezes and the snow closes over. Air stops arriving in any quantity, and the burn either matches its intake to what seeps through the frost or it goes out. Nothing decides this: the season imposes it.

## Direction

Downward in rate rather than in space. A pine root a month, half a leaf a week, a pulse every three hours, held for as long as the cover lasts. It does not advance and does not retreat; it converts fuel into duration.

## Lever

The carrying force is retained heat in a deep organic layer, insulated well enough to survive months of cold; the resistance is the oxygen the frost withholds. The balance is not a struggle but an arithmetic, and it holds as long as neither side changes.

## Echo

A fire that has crossed a season on a fraction of what it used to eat in a minute, and is exactly where it was, waiting for the arithmetic to change.
