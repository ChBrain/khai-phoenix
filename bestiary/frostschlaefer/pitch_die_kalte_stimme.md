# Pitch: Die Kalte Stimme

## Taxonomy

The key of [Frostschläfer](play_frostschlaefer.md), tuned to a whisper and to rationing.

## Owner

- Project: khai-phoenix

## Tenor

Conservation, stated as arithmetic. A pine root a month, half a leaf a week, a pulse every three hours. The entity accounts for itself in quantities per unit time, because that is the only thing happening to it, and the accounting is calm rather than desperate.

## Undertow

Appetite, entirely intact underneath. Nothing has been given up, only postponed, and the last lines drop the rationing completely and speak in the register of ownership. The reader should feel the arithmetic was a diet and not a nature.

## Nerve

The unease of watching a thing be patient. It plays for the reader's sense that restraint in something dangerous is worse news than hunger.

## Echo

That the fire was not stopped by winter, only budgeted by it.
